## ⚙️ **Detailed Features**

### **🎯 Aimbot**
- **Humanized Smooth Aim** – Natural, undetectable movement
- **Visibility Check** – Only targets you can see
- **Recoil Control** – Compensates weapon kick patterns
- **Aimlock** – Locks onto target until manually released
- **Predict** – Compensates for target movement
- **Save Target** – Quickly re-target last enemy
- **Macros** – Customizable keybinds for instant actions

### **👁️ Wallhack & ESP**
- **Wallhack** – See through walls with adjustable transparency
- **Player ESP**:
  - **Boxes** – Bounding boxes
  - **Lines** – Aim lines to players
  - **Skeleton** – Detailed bone tracking
  - **Health** – Current HP indicators
  - **Groggy** – Knocked state detection
  - **Nickname** – Player names
  - **Distance** – Distance to target
  - **Out Screen** – ESP for off-screen players
- **Loot ESP** – Track weapons, armor, and items

### **🗺️ Radar**
- Fully customizable position and size
- Team color coding
- Adjustable range

### **⚙️ Customization**
- **Hotkeys** – Assign any key to features
- **Draw Settings** – Adjust ESP colors, thickness, and visibility
- **Configuration System** – Save/load presets
